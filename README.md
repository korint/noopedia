# Noopedia

**Noopedia is a concept and an evolving research project proposing a new approach to the organization of human knowledge.**

Inspired by Vladimir I. Vernadsky's ideas about scientific thought as a planetary phenomenon and the noosphere, Noopedia explores how humanity might organize knowledge as a shared, interconnected, continuously developing structure that helps advance further inquiry.

Its guiding principles are **unity, continuity, and commonality of knowledge**. Noopedia is not limited to an encyclopedia, a particular data model, or a technical system. The concept exists independently of the present state of its implementation; its future forms, methods, and consequences remain open to investigation.

> **Guiding question:** How can humanity organize the accumulation, preservation, interconnection, and continuous development of knowledge so that the resulting knowledge structure itself helps advance further understanding?

### Current research: TS001

**TS001 — Cross-Source Knowledge-to-Narrative Assembly** investigates whether coherent, source-traceable texts can be composed from unordered knowledge atoms drawn from different sources. Its ten subtests, evaluation criteria, and future findings are tracked in [Research Tests and Recorded Results](docs/RESEARCH_TESTS_AND_RESULTS.md).

**Status: `NOT_RUN`.** A defined test plan is not evidence of successful execution.
