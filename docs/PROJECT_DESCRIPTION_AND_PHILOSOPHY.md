# Noopedia — Project Description, Purpose, and Philosophy

## 1. An open research concept

**Noopedia is a concept and an evolving research project proposing a new approach to the organization of human knowledge.**

Influenced by **Vladimir I. Vernadsky's** ideas of scientific thought as a planetary phenomenon and the noosphere, Noopedia seeks to rethink how humanity **accumulates, preserves, interconnects, investigates, criticizes, and develops knowledge**.

The proposed approach is **not final**. Its principles, methods, architecture, and possible forms of realization are subjects of research. They may change as the idea develops, as evidence accumulates, and as new technical or intellectual possibilities emerge.

**Noopedia already exists as a proposed concept independently of the progress of its technical implementation.** A functioning common human scientific apparatus is an aspiration, not a prerequisite for proposing this new direction of inquiry. At its present stage, the project's principal contribution is **the formulation of a problem and a possible direction for investigating it**, not a claim to have completed the system.

The concept may be fruitful even if its eventual realization differs substantially from present designs, or if independent projects adopt, transform, or surpass some of its ideas. Its value is therefore not confined to a single codebase, institution, application, or delivery milestone.

### The guiding question

> **How can humanity organize the accumulation, preservation, interconnection, and continuous development of knowledge so that the resulting knowledge structure itself helps advance further understanding?**

### What Noopedia seeks to become

Noopedia explores the possibility of **a shared, interconnected, and continuously developing space of human knowledge**, where the results of inquiry, their evidence, relationships, criticism, corrections, historical interpretations, and unresolved questions can become part of one continuing process. Such a structure might help connect discoveries across disciplines and generations and generate further questions and investigations.

**Noopedia is not merely an encyclopedia.** An encyclopedia article can be one form of presenting knowledge, not the definition of the project. **Nor is Noopedia identical to the noosphere.** Drawing on Vernadsky, it seeks to contribute a possible form of organizing humanity's scientific thought and, potentially, a factor in the noosphere's further evolution. That possibility is a research hypothesis, not a proven outcome.

The early technical directions—knowledge atomization and molecular assembly, MPOV, Noo-View, Noo Atlas, and human/AI collaboration—are **exploratory instruments**, not permanent limits of Noopedia's identity. Initial processing may be manual, automated, or mixed. What matters is the development of a common, inspectable space of knowledge that remains open to future methods and contributions.

**Its future forms, methods, and consequences remain open.** For a concise public statement and credits, see [About Noopedia](ABOUT.md).

## 2. Vernadsky and the noosphere: the intellectual foundation

In §61 of *Scientific Thought as a Planetary Phenomenon*, Vernadsky describes scientific facts and observations, regardless of where or by whom they originate, entering a **unified scientific apparatus**, being classified and brought into common form, and becoming available for criticism, reflection, and further scientific work.

> Every scientific fact and every scientific observation, wherever and by whomever it has been made, enters a unified scientific apparatus. There it is classified and brought into a common form, and immediately becomes a shared resource for criticism, reflection, and scientific work.

*Project translation of the Russian passage drawn from the Russian passage; not an official published English translation. The original appears in Vernadsky, [*Научная мысль как планетное явление*, §61](https://infoliolib.ru/philos/vernadsky/nmysl3.html). “Shared resource” refers to scientific accessibility, not an automatic waiver of source rights.*

**Noopedia seeks to become a contemporary form of such an apparatus**, not merely an auxiliary tool that makes isolated pieces of it machine-processable. This is a continuity of intellectual **purpose**, not a claim that Vernadsky prescribed today's databases, knowledge graphs, AI models, or interfaces.

The same paragraph's broader context emphasizes the freedom to pursue science and the dissemination of knowledge. Noopedia therefore needs to make knowledge available for challenge, correction, reflection, and new research, not merely classify claims or publish a single official narrative.

See [Vernadsky, the Noosphere, and a Common Human Scientific Apparatus](VERNADSKY_NOOSPHERE_AND_SCIENTIFIC_APPARATUS.md) for the full philosophical argument, sources, and limits of attribution.

## 3. Three foundational ideas

Unity, continuity, and commonality are the present foundational principles of the research direction. They give the project continuity without being immune to investigation, refinement, or criticism.

### Unity of knowledge

Human knowledge can become an **interconnected whole**, while retaining its sources, contextual limits, disciplinary distinctions, languages, and divergent interpretations. Unity does not mean forced uniformity or one final point of view.

### Continuity of knowledge

Scientific observations, inherited knowledge, corrections, refinements, discarded explanations, new evidence, and questions should remain part of a **continuous, inspectable history**. Corrections to initial human or machine interpretations should preserve what changed and why, and may guide subsequent processing and updates.

### Commonality of knowledge

Knowledge is created through the **collective efforts of humanity**. Noopedia should make its organized results useful and accessible for further human investigation, criticism, reuse, and extension, subject to legitimate source rights, safety, and governance constraints.

**Unity · Continuity · Commonality** express the project's fundamental aim: make the accumulation, preservation, criticism, and further development of shared human knowledge a consciously organized process.

## 4. Not only preserving knowledge, but enabling new knowledge

A common knowledge structure can potentially reveal relationships between domains that individual documents do not make apparent. It can connect observations, historical interpretations, evidence, contradictions, and unresolved questions to new lines of inquiry.

**This is a research ambition, not a proven scientific outcome.** Better organization of knowledge could change the conditions under which new knowledge is produced. Noopedia's significance would then extend beyond access to existing information: the structure itself could assist investigation and discovery.

It is unnecessary to impose a final architecture or a fixed definition of the project's future. Human knowledge develops continuously; Noopedia's methods and representations should be capable of changing with it. **The project’s identity can survive changes in technology:** future systems may replace today’s atomization methods, knowledge graphs, spherical views, or article compilers without replacing the commitment to unity, continuity, and commonality of knowledge. The principles give direction and remain open to reasoned revision; current mechanisms are revisable research instruments.

## 5. People, technologies, and the work of interpretation

Noopedia's essence is **not machine atomization**. The initial organization and interpretation of source material can be performed by students, researchers, editors, volunteers, software, AI, or combinations of people and technical means. What matters is the ability to integrate responsibly processed results into an interconnected, examinable and evolving body of knowledge.

AI may be a powerful tool, but neither LLMs nor today's atom–molecule model defines the outer limits of Noopedia. Human corrections must be possible and traceable, with the reasoning and source context preserved and, where appropriate, incorporated into future processing.

The envisaged Noopedia@home volunteer network is one proposed way to distribute computational work and verification, not the only possible form of human participation.

## 6. Why the project needs an evidence-aware structure

Information is abundant, but the relationship between *what a source says*, *what a reader understands*, *what can be independently justified*, and *what a generated article presents as fact* is often obscured. A conventional article is useful for reading, but its prose can entangle assertions, interpretations, citations, uncertainty, and editorial priorities. Repeating a statement in many derivative texts can make it appear better supported than it actually is.

Noopedia seeks to make those distinctions visible and computationally usable. It does not treat every source statement as a truth about the world, and it does not infer consensus merely because content is repeated. It should retain the paths by which assertions were recorded, interpreted, compared, selected, challenged, and revised.

A further motivation is that generative AI can produce convincing text without establishing the truth of its contents. Noopedia therefore treats **traceability, semantic fidelity, epistemic restraint, and inspectable provenance** as foundational requirements rather than optional citation decorations.

## 7. A source is not an atom; an atom is not a verified fact

A source may be a scientific paper, historical document, news item, official statement, web page, or other permitted material. The system must preserve the precise source and revision that supports each extracted assertion.

A **knowledge atom** is a reusable unit of meaning grounded in an explicit evidential context. The practical representation may involve the existing **SAS / CIN / SPP** semantic chain:

- **SAS — source assertion:** what an exact version of a source actually states, linked to that source and its evidence.
- **CIN — contextual interpretation:** a bounded, context-sensitive interpretation of the source assertion, including reference resolution and semantic roles where justified.
- **SPP — normalized proposition:** a proposition prepared for meaningful comparison and reuse, subject to the existing validation and admission boundaries.

These are existing project concepts, not an instruction to add new record types. The precise storage and runtime contracts remain governed by the actual repository and applicable accepted specifications.

An atom is **not** automatically a world-verified fact. Its source may be mistaken; the assertion may be quoted, disputed, hypothetical, prospective, evaluative, or conditional. The distinction between **semantic fidelity**, **source support**, and **epistemic warrant** must survive every downstream transformation.

## 8. Deep semantic atomization

Atomization is not mere sentence splitting, named-entity extraction, or creation of subject–predicate–object triples without context. Meaning also appears in verbs, relations, subordinate clauses, temporal constraints, negation, attribution, modality, and other constructions. An operation that extracts only facts about the main subject of a document can miss separately useful knowledge about other people, events, or places mentioned in that document.

The goal is to preserve independently reusable source-supported content **without manufacturing details the source does not establish**. A description of a construction does not, by itself, tell us who built it, the exact beginning and end dates, or the materials used. Such absent values may motivate **questions**, but they are not new facts or placeholder answers.

The project distinguishes three operations:

1. **Preserve what is supported by evidence in a defined source scope.**
2. **Derive meaningful, applicable questions from known structures and relationships.**
3. **Show which answers are absent or unresolved within that explicit scope.**

These operations must not be silently collapsed. An unanswered question is not a negative fact, and absence in one source is not universal absence.

## 9. Knowledge molecules: context-specific recombination

A **molecule** is a meaningful composition of related atoms for a particular purpose. Several views may reuse the same atoms and preserve the same origin and evidence trail. Their relative importance is not an intrinsic, permanent property of the atoms.

A template may specify an output goal, audience, amount of detail, kinds of relevant relations, coverage needs, ordering constraints, and rules for showing uncertainty and disagreement. A request for an encyclopedia article, a historical timeline, a scientific overview, or a spatial display may lead to **different selections, groupings, priorities, and orders** over the same knowledge base.

Thus **ranking is a property of contextual assembly, not a global truth score**. A molecule's role depends on how its atoms relate, not simply on the sum of individual atom scores. Presentation order may follow chronology, causality, explanation, or spatial relations; the highest-priority item need not be shown first.

Selection and ranking must never alter the underlying claims, create fictitious corroboration, strip essential qualifiers, or bury important contradictions. The system should record why a representation selected or omitted elements and which template/version and source versions it used.

## 10. One knowledge base, many possible expressions

**Knowledge must not inherit the source's order of exposition as an obligatory property of its own structure. Its representation should be formed according to purpose, context, and semantic relationships, while preserving those sequences and dependencies that are part of the meaning itself.**

An atom does not become permanently “third” because it appeared in the source's third paragraph. A timeline, a technical explanation, an encyclopedia entry, and a history of changing interpretations may select and order the same grounded knowledge differently. However, an event's chronology, a proof's dependencies, a quoted speaker's identity, or a novel's deliberate sequence of revelations can themselves be meaningful. Those relations must not be erased by reordering.

**The original remains indispensable.** Its exact words, authorship, composition, edition, and permitted source access must be distinguishable from extracted meaning. Generated descriptions are **new source-grounded compositions**, not a lossless reconstruction of the author's text. Material claims must remain traceable to specific source versions and evidence passages; public access to the original, when possible, must respect rights and access restrictions.

For the full principles and research questions, see [Original Sources, Atomization, and Narrative Assembly](SOURCES_ATOMIZATION_AND_NARRATIVE_ASSEMBLY.md).

Noopedia's proposed flow is:

```text
Permitted source and its exact revision
    → source-supported assertions (SAS)
    → justified contextual interpretations (CIN)
    → comparable propositions (SPP) and provenance/relations
    → controlled selection for a purpose and template
    → knowledge molecules and a presentation plan
    → article / comparison / timeline / Noo-View / Noo Atlas / other output
    → check semantic and epistemic integrity against original evidence
```

This is a **conceptual map**. It does not assert that every arrow is currently automatic, production-ready, or implemented by the same software component. The accepted technical gates, source rights, identity constraints, publication/review status, and actual repository behavior are separate verification matters.

Assembling an article is not simply running extraction in reverse. It entails subject selection, coverage, dependency ordering, and controlled expression. A generated text or view does not become an independent original source just because it is published or consumed in another view.

## 11. MPOV: different projections of the same subject

**MPOV (Multiple Points of View)** is the ability to examine a subject through distinct **situated perspectives or projections** of the same shared knowledge structure. These may reflect states, societies, cultures, disciplines, institutions, scientific schools, or historical moments. MPOV is **not** primarily about conspiracy theories, marginal claims, or treating every narrative as equally true.

For example, an event in the Cold War or space race can be viewed from the **USSR** and **US** perspectives. The projections may draw on different source traditions, questions, institutional interests, descriptions, and points of emphasis while preserving the common event, its chronology, and each statement's evidence. A third, comparative view can explore what is shared, what differs, and what remains uncertain. Neither country is one homogeneous voice; specific claims require specific actors and sources.

A perspective can change **selection, grouping, emphasis, and presentation**; it must not change the original meaning of a claim, invent group beliefs, erase evidence, or turn an attributed position into an independently established fact. Disagreement is possible, but **not a prerequisite** for useful MPOV.

The earlier layer-model specification explicitly describes *Western / Soviet / Local / Consensus* lenses, generated as read-time selections rather than wholly separate truth stores. That is a dated design reference, not proof of current implementation.

See [MPOV — Multiple Perspectives on Shared Knowledge](MPOV_MULTIPLE_PERSPECTIVES.md) for the canonical conceptual clarification and verification criteria.

## 12. How humanity’s understanding of a subject evolves

**Noopedia articles should describe not only a subject itself but also, where relevant and source-supported, how humanity has understood, explained, debated, and reinterpreted that subject.** This is a defining article goal **alongside MPOV, atomization, and visualization in semantic space, including spherical Noo-View representations**.

The **history of understanding** is not the same as the history of the object, a history of source edits, or Noopedia's own record history. An article on Mars, for example, may need to connect historical accounts and hypotheses with later observations and contemporary open questions without treating a formerly popular idea as a present-day established fact.

**MPOV and this history are complementary:** MPOV offers different perspectives, including those existing at the same time; the history of understanding follows how those perspectives and explanations arise, coexist, change, and interact across time. A significant change needs its own evidence, not a fabricated linear story of inevitable progress.

Article assembly should explicitly consider this dimension when relevant evidence exists, without inventing an intellectual history for evidence-poor subjects. See [Evolution of Understanding in Encyclopedic Articles](EVOLUTION_OF_UNDERSTANDING_IN_ARTICLES.md).

## 13. Knowledge has spatial, temporal, and semantic dimensions

Noopedia's research vision includes complementary views:

- **Noo-View:** exploring entities, events, propositions, evidence, contradictions, and questions as meaningful relationships, including graph, radial, spherical, or temporal representations. A line should have a defined relation; visual proximity alone is not proof of semantic affinity.
- **Geo-View / Noo Atlas:** linking knowledge to locations, territories, planets, or other geographic contexts when relevant and evidence permits. Geospatial layers and astronomical or planetary representations require their own data, coordinate, and provenance controls.
- **Time:** tracing how subjects, source statements, accepted knowledge, and interpretations develop or change.

The physical visualization metaphor is not a substitute for scientific validation. A 3D sphere does not prove that the underlying epistemic or semantic relations are correct; a useful interface must expose those distinctions.

## 14. Noopedia and the open future of the noosphere

Vernadsky's account of scientific thought and the noosphere is the project's intellectual origin. **Noopedia aspires to become a factor in the further evolution of the noosphere**, because changing how shared knowledge is organized could change how people investigate, collaborate, and create knowledge.

This is an **open conceptual hypothesis**, not a prediction of an inevitable outcome. Neither the future of the noosphere nor the eventual form of Noopedia is settled. It would be premature to insist that Noopedia must, or cannot, amount to some particular "digital embodiment" of the noosphere.

```text
Vernadsky: scientific thought as a planetary phenomenon
       ↓
Noopedia: shared human scientific apparatus
       unity · continuity · commonality
       ↓
Potential new capacities for collective inquiry
       ↓
Possible contributions to the evolution of the noosphere
```

Noopedia is not the noosphere itself, nor does it claim to have achieved the future influence it seeks. **Its role is a developing process and a possible contribution to that wider planetary phenomenon.** The initial architecture may one day be replaced by more powerful techniques. **What should persist is the aim: a common space of human knowledge that humanity continuously creates, examines, corrects, and develops.** This identity is defined by the enduring research purpose and three foundational principles, rather than by today’s database schema, atom format, rendering technology, or even the familiar form of an encyclopedia.

## 15. Research before claims of completeness

The project should be willing to show partial progress honestly: limited fixtures, unresolved referents, withheld assertions, incomplete coverage, false positives found by tests, and bounded experiments. A demonstration should distinguish **what the system actually did** from **what the architecture intends eventually to do**.

Public explanation is part of the work. Short accessible demonstrations can make the research intelligible without pretending a complete encyclopedia has been delivered. The example of one passage being atomized and reused in two different outputs is more persuasive than an unsupported statement that the entire web has become structured knowledge.

An illustrative passage about the Eiffel Tower can yield separate source-supported relationships to Paris, a world's fair, a year, and a namesake. The exact split depends on the semantic and evidential contract. Missing builder identities or dates remain explicit questions; they are not supplied by a template or a language model's general knowledge.

An early, bounded empirical question is whether independently sourced, unordered knowledge atoms can support multiple coherent and traceable narratives. [TS001](RESEARCH_TESTS_AND_RESULTS.md) formalizes this question as ten subtests with an explicit obligation to record all conclusions—positive, negative, or inconclusive—in the main project documentation. **It has not yet been run.** This test concerns one proposed mechanism, not the definition or validity of Noopedia's wider philosophical concept.

## 16. The project’s engineering ethic

The implementation should be as simple as the demonstrated requirements allow. First look for existing data structures, contracts, validation paths, and product surfaces. Prefer **a shared semantic core with source-dependent interpretation profiles** to independent atomization engines for every genre; introduce a specialization only after counterexamples justify its quality benefit and both present and future maintenance costs.

Models may assist semantic interpretation, selection, and language generation, but they do not by themselves authorize source admission, factual verification, canonical storage mutations, or publication. The established source/version evidence and the existing controlled review/commit mechanisms must remain authoritative.

A new ranking model, storage table, graph backend, durable record kind, or article engine is not a goal in itself. Architectural additions must be justified by concrete limitations, comparison against simpler alternatives, and regression testing.

## 17. Community-scale processing (proposal)

**Noopedia@home** is a proposed SETI@home-inspired network for community-funded and community-executed processing of evidence-linked knowledge. It provides **two equal paths for participation**: contributors may authorize Noopedia to spend a controlled portion of their AI provider budget through centrally managed API credentials, **or** run a small Python worker on their own computer or server, keeping their API credentials locally or using a local model. Both modes can perform candidate extraction or independent verification, and they can be combined on the same task.

The initial reference benchmark is run with **one project-controlled API key**, using frozen input, prompt, model, and schema versions to measure baseline behavior and cost. A **separately source-reviewed golden set** measures correctness; the first model output is not itself the gold standard. Routine tasks receive **two blind, separately identified candidate executions**. When the results agree, the server conducts or commissions an **additional source-grounded verification**, using its own resources or an appropriately authorized contributed API key. Disagreement or unsupported agreement causes HOLD or adjudication, not automatic publication.

This design distributes not only computation but also the cost of verification. Different donated keys do **not** automatically mean independent reasoning if they access the same model; execution and validator diversity must be assessed explicitly. Volunteers never gain authority to bypass existing semantic, provenance, admission, or publication protections. The protocol, Python client, credential broker, and checking network are **proposals, not implemented capabilities**.

See [Noopedia@home — Distributed Knowledge Processing and Independent Verification](DISTRIBUTED_VOLUNTEER_PROCESSING.md) for both participation modes, worker/version protocol, reference calibration, the double-processing-plus-review workflow, and security constraints.

## 18. Openness, stewardship, and unresolved choices

Noopedia is intended as a public research idea that others can inspect, challenge, improve, and potentially build upon independently. Openness of method, provenance, and reproducibility is an important aspiration. **Exact inbound source rights, outbound licensing, access rules, and governance are separate policy decisions**; openness of research does not override copyright or licenses.

Historical project documents include concrete but not necessarily aligned proposals on commercial access, governance, and source acquisition. They remain valuable historical design references, not evidence that one option has been deployed or newly approved by this document. Such choices require explicit project-governance decisions and verification against the actual repository.

## 19. What success would mean

The durable aim is **a shared, interconnected, continuously developing body of human knowledge** that preserves evidence and differences, supports collective investigation, and can generate many inspectable, meaningful representations. A reusable atomic-knowledge substrate is one current technical direction in service of that wider ambition.

Evidence of progress would include:

- atomization that preserves semantic roles, context, modality, and evidence across diverse genres;
- meaningful reuse of atoms across subjects and outputs without re-extracting the original text;
- automatic, template-sensitive molecule formation with good coverage and explainable choices;
- explicit representation of what is supported, disputed, unknown within scope, or merely hypothesized;
- traceable provenance and independent controls against circular or duplicated corroboration;
- reader-visible, source-grounded histories of how understanding of a subject has changed, alongside MPOV and factual history;
- usable semantic, temporal, and geographic visualizations; and
- honest comparisons between intended capabilities and observed, tested implementation behavior.

**The ambition is not to generate more text. It is to help humanity organize, preserve, examine, correct, connect, and expand its common knowledge.**
