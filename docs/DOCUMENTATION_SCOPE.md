# Noopedia — Documentation Scope and Evidence Boundaries

## What the documentation represents

Noopedia is **a proposed concept and an evolving research project**. Its current central contribution is to state a research problem and explore a new direction for organizing human knowledge. The concept exists independently of the state of its software. An implementation can test, extend, or challenge the proposed approach, but its incompleteness does not erase the concept.

The documentation separates three kinds of statements:

1. **Philosophical and research purpose** — what Noopedia asks, proposes, and hopes to investigate.
2. **Design requirements and technical hypotheses** — what the project intends to test, implement, or preserve as an engineering constraint.
3. **Observed implementation** — what actual source code, tests, data, and runtime evidence demonstrate.

No statement in the first two categories should be presented as proof of the third. The documentation alone does not verify the present behavior of any live codebase or deployment.

## Interpretive safeguards

- **Atom structure is preliminary.** Its logical fields and test fixtures do not establish additional physical tables, record types or implemented features.
- **TS001 is not executed in the supplied package.** A registered test and a result template are not outcome evidence.
- **SAS / CIN / SPP** terminology concerns source assertion, contextual interpretation, and normalized proposition respectively. Naming in documents does not establish current code identifiers, database column names, or migrations.
- **Source support is not world verification.** A source can faithfully contain an incorrect claim.
- **MPOV is a contextual projection**, not a license to treat unfounded claims as established truth.
- **History of understanding is not merely a change log.** It concerns how human beliefs, interpretations, and explanations of a subject changed, based on attributable evidence.
- **Entity templates and output templates differ.** A schema of applicable attributes is not the same as a policy for grouping and assembling evidence into a presentation.
- **Original text is not regenerated by atomization.** Source sequence and expression remain source properties; meaningful dependencies remain semantic constraints; output order is chosen for the representation. A source URL is not an authenticated source version or proof of access to its exact passage.
- **The noosphere hypothesis is open.** Noopedia is neither identical to the noosphere nor guaranteed to influence it in any predetermined way.

## Maintaining the main documentation

Keep `/docs/` organized by durable subject, not by release or milestone. Explain the research status plainly without inserting editorial revision logs into thematic documents. New findings should be reflected in the relevant published documents, with evidence and limitations clearly identified.

Source snapshots, evidence lineage, and compatibility protocols may of course require precise **technical revision identifiers**. This is distinct from making public project documentation a succession of dated versions.
