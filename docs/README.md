# Noopedia — Documentation

**Noopedia is a concept and an evolving research project proposing a new approach to the organization of human knowledge.** Its aim is not confined to an encyclopedia or any particular technical system. Inspired by Vernadsky's account of scientific thought and the noosphere, it asks how a shared, evolving structure of knowledge could support further human inquiry.

The idea is meaningful **before its software realization is complete**. Its methods, principles, and possible forms remain open to investigation and change. The three present guiding principles are **unity, continuity, and commonality of knowledge**.
